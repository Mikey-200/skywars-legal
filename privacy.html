SkyWars - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026 · Applies to the SkyWars Android game (package com.mikeey.skywars)

SkyWars is an aircraft combat game made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This page explains what information the game uses, why, who processes it for us, how long it is kept and how you can delete it. We don't sell your information and we don't show ads in the game today.

1. What we collect and why

InformationWhy
A random player ID (created automatically when you first open the game, no login needed)To save your progress online and identify your pilot in online matches.
Your username, pilot emblem, rank and public career statsShown to other players (rooms, chat, friends list, leaderboards, pilot profiles).
Game progress (coins, planes, upgrades, missions, settings, rewards)Stored online so it isn't lost if you change or reset your phone.
Optional SIGN IN WITH GOOGLE (a sign-in token from your Google account, which contains your Google account ID and email address)Only if you choose to sign in, so your pilot comes back on a new or reset phone. Feedback reports you send (SEND FEEDBACK in Settings): your message, its type and rating, plus your username, player ID, game version, phone model, Android version, memory size, graphics setting, frame rate, screen size and the last error the game loggedTo understand and fix what you report. Usage statistics (screens opened, matches played, app version, rough country - via Google Analytics for Firebase, with a random app-instance ID)To see which parts of the game are played and fix what doesn't work.

Technical data (device model, operating system, game version, IP address, error logs)Needed by our service providers to connect you, keep matches working and fix problems.

We don't collect your real name, contacts, photos, precise location or payment card details. Reminder notifications (supply drop ready, weekly missions, etc.) are scheduled on the phone itself and don't send any information to us; news notifications come through Firebase Cloud Messaging.

2. Who processes it for us

The online parts of SkyWars run on Unity Gaming Services by Unity Technologies (Authentication, Cloud Save, Cloud Code, Multiplayer, Friends, Leaderboards, Vivox voice and text chat, Moderation). Unity processes this information for us under its own terms and privacy policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy. Usage statistics, crash reports and news notifications use Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging) by Google, and SIGN IN WITH GOOGLE uses Google's sign-in service: firebase.google.com/support/privacy. The game is distributed through Google Play (policies.google.com/privacy). Information may be processed on servers outside your country.

If we later add in-app purchases or rewarded ads, this policy will be updated before they go live, and the game will ask for any consent that is required.

3. How long we keep it

Your pilot and progress are kept while you play. Chat messages are kept only briefly (the latest 100 world chat messages are visible; a squadron's comms wall keeps its latest 40, and everything about a squadron is deleted or closed when it is disbanded); chat text attached to a report is kept until the report is reviewed. Weekly leaderboards reset every week; past weeks are kept so rewards can be paid. Feedback reports are deleted once they've been dealt with (at the latest after 12 months). Firebase keeps crash reports for 90 days.

4. Deleting your data

In the game: PILOT > ACCOUNT > DELETE. This deletes your pilot, progress, username and online account permanently. You can also ask us to delete it through the contact below - include your username or pilot ID (shown on the PILOT screen).

5. Children

SkyWars is not directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect information from them. Online chat is between players. If you believe a child under 13 has created a pilot, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your choices

You can play the solo missions without using chat or voice, turn voice chat off in Settings, switch off phone notifications (NOTIFICATIONS > PHONE ALERTS), block and report players, and delete your pilot at any time. Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information - contact us.

7. Changes

If this policy changes we'll update the date at the top and, for big changes, tell you in the game's notifications.

8. Contact

In the game: SETTINGS > SEND FEEDBACK (choose OTHER) - it goes straight to the developer. Include your username or pilot ID if it's about your account.
