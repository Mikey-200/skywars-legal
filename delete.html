Delete your SkyWars account

You can permanently delete your SkyWars pilot and everything linked to it at any time. This page explains how, and what happens to your data.

App SkyWarsDeveloper Independent (Mikeey)Package com.mikeey.skywars
On this page
01  Delete in the game 02  Ask us to delete it 03  What gets deleted 04  What may be kept

01Delete in the game

  1. Open SkyWars and go to PILOT.
  2. Open ACCOUNT and tap DELETE.
  3. Confirm. Deletion happens straight away and can't be undone.
Signed in with Google? Deleting the pilot also removes the link between your Google account and SkyWars. Your Google account itself is not affected.

02Ask us to delete it

If you can't open the game any more (for example, it is uninstalled or your phone is lost), reinstall SkyWars and send a request from SETTINGS > SEND FEEDBACK (choose OTHER) with your username or pilot ID. If you signed in with Google, sign in again first so we can find your pilot. We delete it within 30 days.

03What gets deleted

  • Your pilot, username, emblem and online account (player ID)
  • Game progress: coins, planes, upgrades, missions, rewards and ranked rating
  • Settings and HUD layouts backed up online
  • Friends, friend requests and blocks
  • Your squadron membership: you leave your squadron first (if you were its commander, command passes to the next member; if you were the last member, the squadron closes)

04What may be kept

  • Reports made about abuse or cheating are kept until they are reviewed.
  • Feedback reports you sent are deleted once dealt with, at the latest after 12 months.
  • Crash reports in Firebase Crashlytics are anonymous and expire after 90 days.
  • Leaderboard scores you already posted can stay visible until that leaderboard resets (weekly boards reset every week). Ask us and we'll remove them by hand.
  • Chat messages already sent disappear as newer messages replace them (latest 100 in world chat, 40 on a squadron wall).
